KISII UNIVERSITY

ADMISSION FORM

Kisii University is a world class University located in Kenya offering more than ninety eight courses. It follows the 8-4-4 system of education and it is among the best University ranked as best in many schools more so the school of law. Kisii University admits students every September while those others who ae self-sponsered are admitted in different times of the year. The students always come from the whole part of the country to persue a degree, masters, PHD or even more . Our main campus is located in Kisii towm whereas there are other branches including in towms like Migori, Eldoret, Kapenguria. We offer best education facilities for everybody ready to learn.
